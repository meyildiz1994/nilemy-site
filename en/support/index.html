Common questions

Where are my logs kept?

On your phone, encrypted. An account is optional; with one, your logs are encrypted on your phone before they’re backed up. We don’t look at your logs.

Will my logs move to a new phone?

With an account, yes: sign in the same way on the new phone and your logs come back. Without one they don’t move on their own; on your old phone, create an account with Profile › Sign in or create an account, or save your logs as a file in Profile › Privacy and data › Your data.

I forgot my sign-in password.

On the email sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and a reset link is emailed to you.

How do I delete my account?

Profile › Account › Delete account. Your encrypted backup and your account are permanently removed from the server, and your logs and settings are removed from your phone. Save a copy first in Profile › Privacy and data › Your data if you want one. If you can’t open the app, email info@nilemy.com from your account’s email address.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

Profile › Nilemy Premium › Manage subscription, or on iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, on Android Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. If you cancel, Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Why do I see ads?

After the first week, the free version shows Google AdMob ads at the bottom of Home, History and Insights. Ads are never personalised and your logs are never used for ads. To remove them, tap “Remove ads” above an ad, or subscribe in Profile › Nilemy Premium.

What is the “For you today” note?

With Premium, a short note on Home about your day, based on your logs. It’s written by Claude, an AI; it needs an account and for you to turn it on. Your notes, name and sex logs are not sent. Turn it off any time in Profile › Privacy and data › Daily note.

How do I turn on pregnancy mode?

Choose Profile › Tracking mode › I’m pregnant, then enter your due date or your last period. Home shows your week, the days to go and your baby’s size; the kick counter, contraction timer, weight, checkups and hospital bag list are there too. When your pregnancy is over, choose that on the same screen.

How are estimates worked out?

At first, the cycle length you entered is used. Once you’ve logged two cycles, the average of your last six takes over. If your cycles are irregular, you see a range instead of a single date. Estimates are for information only.

I forgot my app passcode.

If Face ID or fingerprint is on, unlock with it, then change your passcode in Profile › Privacy and data › App lock. We don’t know your passcode either; if biometric unlock is off, the only way back in is to delete and reinstall the app, which deletes your logs.

Reminders aren’t arriving.

Check that notifications are allowed for Nilemy in your phone’s Settings › Notifications, then check that Profile › Reminders is on in the app.

How do I change the language?

Nilemy uses your phone’s language: Turkish, English, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil) or Italian. If your phone is set to another language, it opens in English. To change the language for Nilemy alone, go to Settings › Nilemy › Language on iPhone, or Settings › Apps › Nilemy › Language on Android.

How do I delete all my data?

Profile › Privacy and data › Your data › Delete all data removes everything on this phone. If you have an account, use Profile › Account › Delete account to remove your backup too; it also clears this phone. This can’t be undone.

Can I use Nilemy for contraception?

No. Estimates are not a reliable way to prevent pregnancy. Nilemy isn’t a medical device; talk to a healthcare professional about any health concerns.
